# Voice sheet: Wren

## The rule

- *(Settled R0302-03.)* First person, past tense, Wren's. Told as it happens: no "later I learned", no "I did not know yet".
- *(Settled R0302-05.)* Every chapter opens on a day or a tide.
- *(Settled R0302-02.)* Wren notices; she does not explain. Nothing in the narration says why the tide runs late.
- *(Proposal.)* American spelling (the writer's choice). Contractions are fine in dialogue and sparing in narration.

## The register

- *(Proposal.)* Plain by default. Close detail where something holds her: work in skilled hands, the lens, the weather turning, Agnes's hands. A line or a gap for what bores her.
- *(Proposal.)* Comparisons from her own life (the village, her grandmother, school, the house). At most a few per chapter, gathered where she looks hard.

## Samples

- The writer's own pages are the voice: [the oil cart](samples/sample-pages.md). Match it; never copy it. A critic never flags a line from the samples.

## Banned

- The words in [banned.txt](banned.txt): modern words and stock phrases that do not belong in her mouth or her head.
