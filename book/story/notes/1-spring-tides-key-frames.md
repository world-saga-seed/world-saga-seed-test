## Brief: 00700, Caught on the causeway

*Under 250 words, as the dictation workflow asks.*

- **Where it sits:** the spring equinox; Saltby and the causeway; the afternoon low water, a spring tide.
- **State at the start:** Wren in Saltby on an errand; the inspector at the inn; Agnes alone on the holm. Wren has the weed (00300) and "and forty" (00400), and has not joined them.
- **State at the end:** the inspector is safe on the holm, wet to the waist; Wren knows what "and forty" means; Agnes's secret still holds.
- **Reveals:** the reader may learn the tide runs late. The reader must not learn that Agnes knew why all along, unless the writer wants it.
- **Settled beats:** frame 4.
- **Only proposed:** that Wren waits at the third post; that Dell fetches the inspector back with the cart.
- **Questions:**
  1. What is Wren after when she sees the inspector start out: to save him, or to save Agnes from his report? How far would she push him?
  2. When Wren asks about "and forty", what does Agnes want: to keep it hers, or to hand it on?
  3. Does the inspector ever learn whose habit saved him? *(Recommendation: no; it keeps R0302-01.)*
