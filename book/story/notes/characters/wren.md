# Wren Ashby (character sheet)

## At a glance

- Thirteen, from Saltby, apprenticed to the keeper since the autumn. The narrator. ([Who she is](#who-she-is))
- Notices hands, weather and work left undone; does not explain. ([Voice](#voice))
- Covers for Agnes out of fear of the parish, then out of love. ([Her arc](#her-arc))
- Habits: thumb on her skirt; checks the weed. ([Habits](#habits))

## Who she is

- *(Settled R0302-03.)* Thirteen. Her grandmother raised her in Saltby and was taken by the parish in her last spring, when her mind went. Wren came to the holm in the autumn on the schoolmaster's word.
- *(Proposal.)* She reads well, for the village, and is proud of it, and hides that she is proud.

## What she wants

- *(Proposal.)* To keep her place. She has nowhere else.
- *(Proposal.)* Not to see the parish cart again.

## Voice

- *(Settled R0302-03.)* First person, past tense, as it happens. She tells what she saw and did, and what she thought then, never what she learned later.
- *(Proposal.)* Short words. Her comparisons come from the village and the house: her grandmother, the school, the oil and the pony. Never from anything she has not seen.

## Habits

- *(Proposal, from the writer's existing scene.)* Gets oil or soot on her thumb and rubs it on her skirt.
- *(Settled R0302-07.)* Looks at the weed on the third post every time she crosses.
- **Not:** she does not count things aloud (that is nobody's habit in this book), and she does not hum.

## Her arc

1. *(Settled.)* Takes "and forty" for a failing mind, and keeps quiet to protect Agnes.
2. *(Settled R0302-01.)* Learns of the eyes and keeps that too.
3. *(Settled.)* At the crossing, joins the weed and "and forty", and waits.
4. *(Settled R0302-01.)* Reads the log to the inspector, and becomes Agnes's eyes.

## Rules for writing her

- *(Settled R0302-02.)* She is right by attention, not cleverness. She never explains the tide to an adult.
