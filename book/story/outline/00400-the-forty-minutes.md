# The forty minutes (outline)

*Chapter 00400 of [Spring tides](1-spring-tides.md). Before: 00300 The third post. After: 00500 The early dark. Wren narrates, first person, past tense, as it happens. Notes for drafting are in the notes file under the same number.*

## Outline

### 1. The first dry morning

- *(Settled R0302-05.)* Opens on the day: the first dry morning of March, when the lens is cleaned.
- *(Proposal.)* Wren on the step stool inside the lamp, warm water, whiting, the soft leather. Agnes below on the gallery bench with the almanac.
- *(Proposal.)* Agnes's rule for the lens: ring by ring, top down, never back up over a ring you have done.

### 2. "And forty"

- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Agnes reads the week's high waters aloud and adds "and forty" after each one.
- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Wren asks "and forty what?"; Agnes says to mind her ring. No explanation.
- *(Proposal.)* Wren knows the almanac by heart from the winter and knows there is no "and forty" in it.
- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Agnes wipes her hands on her apron before she has touched anything.

### 3. Wren's reading

- *(Proposal.)* Wren takes it for her grandmother's end: the same word tied to every sentence, and the parish coming. She says nothing more.

### 4. The third post

- *(Settled R0302-07.)* Agnes sends her to look at the weed on the third post.
- *(Settled R0302-04.)* The weed says the water has only just gone off; the almanac said an hour ago. Wren does not tell her.
- *(Aim.)* The reader puts the two together before Wren does.
