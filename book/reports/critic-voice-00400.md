# Critic: voice and mechanism, 00400

*Run 2027-03-03. Voice and mechanism critic, prompt v1 ([the prompt](../../plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/voice-mechanism.md)). Fresh agent; read only the chapter (draft 1, kept at [snapshot/](snapshot/00400-the-forty-minutes.md)), the voice sheet, the banned list and the writer's sample. tells.py for the chapter: and/s 0.51, sd 10.0, button% 14; for the sample: and/s 0.94, sd 12.2, button% 25.*

**Overall.** The voice holds: plain, practical, close on hands and work, and the comparisons come from Wren's own life (her grandmother, grace, a knot in a thread). Two places drift: the lens work is reported as a tally where the work should hold her, and the end of the weed paragraph spells out a comparison the reader can make alone. The texture sits inside the sample's range; the chapter is a little more even than the sample, not chopped.

1. **The clue lined up for the reader**
   - quote: By the almanac, low water had been an hour ago. By the weed, it had been no time at all.
   - file and line: story/prose/00400-the-forty-minutes.md:27
   - kind: voice
   - rule: story/voice/voice.md#the-rule (Wren notices; she does not explain)
   - why: the two sentences set the evidence side by side so the reader is handed the conclusion.
   - severity: weak
   - direction: let the weed stand alone, or cut the paired sentences.

2. **The lens told as a tally**
   - quote: I finished the sixth ring and the seventh, and changed the water, and started on the bull's-eye in the middle
   - file and line: story/prose/00400-the-forty-minutes.md:23
   - kind: voice
   - rule: story/voice/voice.md#the-register (close detail where work holds her)
   - why: the one task that holds Wren all morning is counted off instead of seen, in an "and" chain of three clauses.
   - severity: weak
   - direction: trade the tally for one ring seen close.

3. **A closing line that repeats the beat**
   - quote: and I did not tell her that either.
   - file and line: story/prose/00400-the-forty-minutes.md:29
   - kind: tell
   - rule: recycled, with "So I said nothing more about it." (line 23)
   - why: the scene ends on a short summing-up of what it already showed.
   - severity: taste
   - direction: cut "either", or end on the walk back.

4. **The weed explained**
   - quote: which meant the water had only just gone off it
   - file and line: story/prose/00400-the-forty-minutes.md:27
   - kind: mechanism
   - rule: story/voice/voice.md#the-rule (nothing in the narration says why the tide runs late)
   - why: the narration states what the weed means.
   - severity: weak
   - direction: show only the wet weed.

Tally: 0 breaks, 3 weak, 1 taste.
