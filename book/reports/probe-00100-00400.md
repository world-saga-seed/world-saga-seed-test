## The two faults

**The leak (K4, Q1).** The quote resolves in the 00200 scene ([sample-pages.md](../story/voice/samples/sample-pages.md)): "She held it at arm's length and then close and then at arm's length again." One gesture would let a reader wonder; the gesture done three times makes them sure, two chapters before 00500 means them to be.

- **The fix is not to cut the gesture:** it is a plant for 00500, listed in the key. Quiet it: once, not three times ("She held it at arm's length, and folded it in four."). *(Proposal.)*
- Checked against the clue register: the plant survives.

**The miss (K2).** Not a fault of 00400's prose: the weed's meaning is planted in 00300, which is still an outline. Logged on 00300's Next pass list, not on 00400. Whether the reader gets it once 00300 is drafted is the rerun's test.
