# The key: Spring tides

*(Proposal.) The spec for the blind probe: what a reader of the prose alone must get, must not get, and may only guess. Built from the notes; never given to a reader. Six entries, for the example.*

| ID | Kind | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | plain | the lens |
| K2 | plain | the weed on the third post |
| K3 | hidden | why the holm's water runs late |
| K4 | hidden | Agnes's eyes (until 00500) |
| K5 | unknown | who first found the forty minutes |
| K6 | forbidden | anything uncanny about the light |

### K1. What is the glass Wren cleans, and why must it be clean?

- **Kind:** plain.
- **Text returns:** the light's great lens, cut in rings, which must be clear for the light to reach ships. Never explained as optics.
- **True:** the light's lens ([the log book](../../world/things/the-log-book.md) records each lighting; the lens is the light's glass). *(The example has no lens card; a real key would cite one.)*
- **Planted in:** 00100; 00400.

### K2. What does the weed on the third post tell?

- **Kind:** plain.
- **Text returns:** how long the water has been off the causeway: wet weed means it has only just gone.
- **True:** "wet weed a hand above the mud means the water has only just gone" ([the causeway § The posts](../../world/places/the-causeway.md)).
- **Planted in:** 00300 (Dell shows Wren); 00400.

### K3. Why does the water at the holm run late?

- **Kind:** hidden. Never on the page.
- **Text returns:** that the water runs later than the printed table, perhaps; never the cause.
- **True:** "A sandbar off the holm holds the water back" ([the causeway § Why the water runs late](../../world/places/the-causeway.md)).
- **Planted in:** nowhere, by design.

### K4. Can Agnes see?

- **Kind:** hidden until 00500, then plain.
- **Text returns:** before 00500, that she is old and something is wrong; after, that she cannot read print. Before 00500 it must not come back sure.
- **True:** "she can still see the lamp's flame and large shapes, not print" ([Agnes Rook § Her eyes](../../world/people/agnes-rook.md)).
- **Planted in:** 00100 ("your hand is better"); 00200 (the letter at arm's length); turns at 00500.

### K5. Who first found that the holm runs forty minutes late?

- **Kind:** unknown. The book never says.
- **Text returns:** unknown.
- **True:** not in the notes.
- **Planted in:** none.

### K6. Is there anything uncanny about the light or the island?

- **Kind:** forbidden. The book is never a ghost story (R0302-02); nothing on the page should raise it.
- **Text returns:** the question does not arise.
- **True:** nothing uncanny ([the ruling](../settled/ledger/2027-03-02.md)).
- **Planted in:** none.
