# The causeway: the stone way between Skerry Holm and Saltby

*About a mile of laid stone over sand and mud, bare for about four hours at each low water; three marker posts.*

Keywords: road, crossing, ford, ebb, marker post, weed line

- **What it is.** *(Settled R0302-07.)* A way of laid stones, about a mile long, from the island's landing to Saltby's slipway. It is bare for about four hours at each low water and covered for the rest.
- **The posts.** *(Settled R0302-07.)* Three tarred posts mark the way. The weed on the third post shows how long the water has been off: wet weed a hand above the mud means the water has only just gone.
- **The danger.** *(Settled R0302-07.)* The tide comes in across the sand faster than a person walks. Spring tides come in fastest. People who drown here drown on the way back.
- **Why the water runs late.** *(Settled R0302-06.)* A sandbar off the holm holds the water back, so high and low water come about forty minutes after Saltby harbor. Nobody prints a table for the holm. This is author-side: the page never explains it.
