# The log book: the keeper's record of every watch

*The Board's ruled book in which each watch is entered: lamp lit, wick trimmed, weather, ships seen.*

Keywords: logbook, entries, ledger, journal

- **What it is.** *(Settled R0302-08.)* A ruled book issued by the Board, one line per watch: the time the lamp was lit and put out, wick trims, oil used, weather, ships seen. It sits on the shelf in the lamp room beside the Board's printed Instructions to Keepers.
- **Who writes it.** *(Settled R0302-03.)* Wren, since the winter, at Agnes's word.
- **Why it matters.** *(Settled R0302-08.)* The inspector reads it first. A log in a second hand is allowed if the keeper signs it.
- **Appears in:** 00100, 00500, 00800.
