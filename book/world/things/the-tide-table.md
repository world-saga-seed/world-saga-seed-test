# The tide table: the almanac printed in Saltby

*A yearly almanac of high and low water for Saltby harbor; correct for the harbor, about forty minutes early for the holm.*

Keywords: tides, timetable, calendar, ebb, flood

- **What it is.** *(Settled R0302-06.)* A thin almanac printed each year in Saltby, giving high and low water for the harbor, day by day. Dell brings the new one each January with the oil.
- **The clue.** *(Settled R0302-06.)* It is right for the harbor and wrong for the holm by about forty minutes ([the causeway](../places/the-causeway.md)). Agnes corrects it aloud; the inspector trusts it as printed.
- **On the page.** *(Settled R0302-04.)* Never said outright. The reader can work it out from "and forty", the weed on the third post, and the inspector's crossing. Never trim a line that carries it.
- **Appears in:** 00100, 00400, 00700.
