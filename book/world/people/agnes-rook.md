# Agnes Rook: the keeper of the light on Skerry Holm

*Keeper for thirty-one years, widowed, losing her sight and hiding it; reads every tide aloud with "and forty".*

Keywords: Mrs. Rook, blind, sight, cataract, old woman

- **Who.** *(Settled R0302-01.)* Keeper of the Skerry Holm light for thirty-one years; her husband kept it before her and died of a fall from the gallery. In her sixties.
- **Her eyes.** *(Settled R0302-01.)* Clouding in both eyes; she can still see the lamp's flame and large shapes, not print. She has told no one. The Board retires a keeper who cannot read the log.
- **Habits.** *(Settled R0302-06.)* Says "and forty" after every high water she reads from the almanac: the island's water runs about forty minutes behind Saltby harbor, and she has added it for thirty years without thinking. Wipes her hands on her apron before she touches the lens, even when they are clean. **Not:** she never counts aloud, and never explains a habit.
- **On the page.** *(Proposal.)* Seen only through Wren: her hands, her voice, the way she holds a page at arm's length and then close.
- **With Wren.** *(Settled R0302-03.)* Asks Wren to write the log "because your hand is better", which is the cover for her eyes.
- **Appears in:** 00100 to 00800.
- **Open:** what she says when Wren asks about "and forty" (the writer dictates it, 00700).
