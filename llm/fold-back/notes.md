# The forty minutes (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **What the scene does:** plants "and forty" and the late weed in one quiet morning, so the reader can solve the tide before Wren does.
- **Cast on the page:** Wren, Agnes.
- **Length:** 3,500 to 4,500 words in the full draft; the pilot in `story/prose/` is a 500-word opening.
- **Pacing:** a lull on purpose. The work is slow and the talk is short. Close detail on the lens, because the work holds Wren; one line for the wind.
- **Clues (never trim):** "And forty." (each time); the weed wet a hand above the mud; "By the almanac, low water had been an hour ago."
