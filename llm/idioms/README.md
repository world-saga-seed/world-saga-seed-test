# Out-of-world phrase finder

The out-of-world phrase finder (kit: plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/idioms.md, Sonnet) lists phrases in the narration that only make sense outside the narrator's world, with a phrasing from her own. The five plants in [chapter.md](chapter.md) test one modern word (parked), two machines (the wireless, a loop), an idiom (zipped my lip) and a film term (slow motion), all in Wren's narration around 1890. It reads only [narration.md](narration.md), the chapter with its dialogue stripped, line for line, so its line numbers are the chapter's. Paste [run.md](run.md) into a fresh Sonnet session in the repo's top folder; never show it [answers.md](answers.md) or chapter.md.

Score a run: `python3 llm/score.py llm/idioms/answers.md runs/idioms/report.md`.
