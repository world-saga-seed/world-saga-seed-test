# Voice and mechanism critic

The voice and mechanism critic (kit: plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/voice-mechanism.md, Sonnet) is the broadest critic: voice, how things work told where they should be shown, model tells, hindsight, register and measures, with clues and held facts guarded. The five plants in [chapter.md](chapter.md) test a stock "It was not X. It was Y." beat, a comparison from outside Wren's life, the narrator lining up the clue for the reader, a "later I learned" line, and the sandbar's cause leaked. It reads the chapter, book/story/voice/pov.md, voice.md, banned.txt and samples/, the causeway and tide-table cards (only to recognize a cause), book/plan/reader-tests/answers.md, [tells.txt](tells.txt) (tools/tells.py, pasted into the prompt) and [reread-hooks.md](reread-hooks.md), the smallest clue register, invented here because the book keeps its clues in its notes. The book has no sound spans and no neighboring drafted chapters, so the sound-span check and the joins are not tested. Paste [run.md](run.md) into a fresh Sonnet session in the repo's top folder; never show it [answers.md](answers.md).

Score a run: `python3 llm/score.py llm/voice-mechanism/answers.md runs/voice-mechanism/report.md`.
