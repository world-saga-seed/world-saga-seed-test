# Setup and belief critic

The setup and belief critic (kit: plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/setup-belief.md, Sonnet) asks what turns up when the story needs it with no cause, what points back to a showing no page has, and where a person acts against what they have lived and believe. The four plants in [chapter.md](chapter.md) test two setups (a lesson from the inspector no page shows, a stranger who hands Wren the secret) and two beliefs (Agnes confessing her eyes, Wren choosing to call the parish she fears). It reads the chapter, the cards in book/world/, Wren's sheet book/story/notes/characters/wren.md, Agnes's card, [firstuse-00400.md](firstuse-00400.md) (tools/firstuse.py) and [knowledge-00400.md](knowledge-00400.md) (tools/knowledge.py); never the outline or notes. Paste [run.md](run.md) into a fresh Sonnet session in the repo's top folder; never show it [answers.md](answers.md).

Score a run: `python3 llm/score.py llm/setup-belief/answers.md runs/setup-belief/report.md`.
