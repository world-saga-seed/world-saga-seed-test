# The lens: the light's great glass, and how it is cleaned

*Invented for this test section: the book has no lens card yet (its reader key says so, K1). The smallest card that sets the cleaning step by step, written to agree with the unfaulted chapter.*

Keywords: lens, glass, rings, bull's-eye, whiting, cleaning

- **What it is.** *(Proposal.)* The light's glass, cut in rings round a bull's-eye in the middle, set inside the lamp above the lamp-room floor. The keeper's apprentice stands on the step stool to reach the top rings.
- **When.** *(Settled R0302-05.)* On the first dry morning of each month; never in damp, which streaks the whiting.
- **Before the glass is touched.** *(Proposal.)* The lamp is put out at sunrise and the glass left to cool. Water never goes on a warm lens: it cracks the rings.
- **The tools.** *(Proposal.)* A bowl of warm water, a jar of whiting, the soft leather.
- **The order.** *(Proposal.)* Ring by ring, top down. Never go back up over a ring that is done: what comes off a lower ring runs down, and a ring gone back to is smeared again.
- **Each ring.** *(Proposal.)* Wet the leather, dip it in the whiting, work the ring until the bloom comes off on the cloth; let the whiting dry white in the cuts, then rub it off.
- **The bull's-eye.** *(Proposal.)* Last. Change the water before it: the gray water from the rings leaves a film on it, and it shows every smear.
