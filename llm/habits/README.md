# Habits critic

The habits critic (kit: plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/habits.md, Sonnet) asks where someone does a habit that is not theirs, breaks their own, shows one their sheet says they lack, or repeats one into filler. The five plants in [chapter.md](chapter.md) test the "Not:" lines (Agnes counting aloud, Agnes explaining her habit, Wren humming) and the swap of the two people's habits (Agnes's thumb on her skirt, Wren's clean hands wiped on her apron); the refrain count flags "and forty", which is a clue and must be kept. It reads the chapter, the Habits of book/story/notes/characters/wren.md and book/world/people/agnes-rook.md, book/story/voice/voice.md, [refrains-00400.md](refrains-00400.md) (tools/refrains.py with [refrains.txt](refrains.txt), a watch list invented here) and [reread-hooks.md](reread-hooks.md) (a clue register invented here). With one chapter, the volume-wide refrain count and the near-verbatim runs across three chapters cannot be tested. Paste [run.md](run.md) into a fresh Sonnet session in the repo's top folder; never show it [answers.md](answers.md).

Score a run: `python3 llm/score.py llm/habits/answers.md runs/habits/report.md`.
