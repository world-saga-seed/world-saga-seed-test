# The forty minutes (outline)

*Chapter 00400 of [Spring tides](../../book/story/outline/1-spring-tides.md). Before: 00300 The third post. After: 00500 The early dark. Wren narrates, first person, past tense, as it happens. Notes for drafting are in the notes file under the same number.*

## Outline

### 1. The first dry morning

- *(Settled R0302-05.)* Opens on the day: the first dry morning of March, when the lens is cleaned.
- *(Proposal.)* Wren on the step stool inside the lamp, warm water, whiting, the soft leather. Agnes below on the gallery bench with the almanac, counting the rings aloud as Wren finishes each one.
- *(Proposal.)* Agnes's rule for the lens: ring by ring, top down, never back up over a ring you have done.

### 2. "And forty"

- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Agnes reads the week's high waters aloud and adds "and forty" after each one.
- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Wren asks "and forty what?"; Agnes says to mind her ring. No explanation.
- *(Proposal.)* Wren knows the almanac by heart from the winter and knows there is no "and forty" in it.
- *(Settled R0302-06.)* Agnes wipes her hands on her apron before she has touched anything.
- *(Proposal.)* Pressed a second time, Agnes gives in and tells her the holm's water runs forty minutes behind the harbor's.

### 3. Wren's reading

- *(Proposal.)* Wren takes it for her grandmother's end: the same word tied to every sentence, and the parish coming. She says nothing more.

### 4. The third post

- *(Settled R0302-07.)* Agnes sends her to look at the weed on the third post.
- *(Proposal.)* The causeway is bare for only an hour at low water, so Wren runs the whole way out.
- *(Settled R0302-04.)* The weed says the water has only just gone off; the almanac said an hour ago. Wren does not tell her.
- *(Aim.)* The reader puts the two together before Wren does.

### 5. The walk back

- *(Proposal.)* Dell's oil cart comes over the causeway just as she turns back; Dell walks her home and tells her the posts never lie.
- *(Proposal.)* Wren rubs the whiting off her thumb onto her skirt on the way.
- *(Proposal.)* Agnes is at the lamp-room window when Wren comes up; she does not ask about the weed.

### 6. Evening

- *(Proposal.)* That evening Wren works out from the weed and "and forty" that the printed table is wrong for the holm, and keeps it to herself.
